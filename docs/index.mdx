# Custom parts & CAD

Exported CAD for the custom mechanical parts on the chair. Files live in the
[`rammp-hardware`](https://github.com/rammp-org/rammp-hardware) repo; the links
below download them directly.

## Sensor puck

The wrist sensor puck mounts between the Kinova Gen3 arm and the gripper and
exposes I/O routed through the arm. It is described on
[RAMMP Gen 1.5](/platform#kinova-wrist-puck-attachment). STEP models, by Kinova
part number:
